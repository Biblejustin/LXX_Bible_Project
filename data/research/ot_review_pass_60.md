# OT Review Pass 60

Scope: small article cleanup for visible place, body, and earth idioms.

Applied:
- `over face of` -> `over the face of`
- `make atonement to face of your God` -> `make atonement before the face of your God`
- `from root of` -> `from the root of`
- `to/from top of` -> `to/from the top of`
- `at/from corner of` -> `at/from the corner of`
- `corner of house` -> `corner of the house`
- `in/through middle of` -> `in/through the middle of`
- `middle of tent/portion` -> `middle of the tent/portion`
- `from/in/at entrance of` -> `from/in/at the entrance of`
- `entrance of new gate/sea` -> `entrance of the new gate/sea`
- `to/from door of` -> `to/from the door of`
- `door of furnace` -> `door of the furnace`
- `gate of city` -> `gate of the city`
- `court of prison` -> `court of the prison`
- `mouth of den/eater` -> `mouth of the den/eater`
- `all earth` -> `all the earth`

Rationale:
- These are high-confidence English article omissions with a small reviewable scope.
- Avoided high-frequency constructs such as `sons of`, `hands of`, and `day of` because they need context review.
