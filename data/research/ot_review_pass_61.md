# OT Review Pass 61

Scope: small article cleanup for remaining idiomatic body/place phrases.

Applied:
- `heart of sea` -> `heart of the sea`
- `set your heart as heart of god` -> `set your heart as the heart of a god`
- `in/into ears of` -> `in/into the ears of`
- `from sound of` -> `from the sound of`
- `heard in sea` -> `heard in the sea`
- `from breast(s)` -> `from the breast(s)`
- `in upper court` -> `in the upper court`
- `in fire of` -> `in the fire of`
- `in streets of` -> `in the streets of`
- Isaiah 40:12 exact cleanup for `with hand`, `with span`, `by handful`, `with scale`, and `with balance`.

Rationale:
- These are bounded English article/idiom fixes surfaced by the pass 61 scan.
- Avoided broad `hand(s) of`, `sons of`, and proper-name construct phrases because they need separate review.
