# OT Review Pass 40

Scope = cross-reference watch cleanup exposed by Brenton filename discovery.

Status keys:
- `keep` = leave wording
- `keep-provisional` = leave for now, but still open
- `needs-logos` = pause for deeper lexical / apparatus check
- `needs-remap` = source verse-map / structural issue, not simple wording issue
- `revised` = wording changed in source or tracked review note

## Jeremiah 22:3
- status: `revised`
- change: smoothed the imperative chain for judgment, rescue, oppression, impiety, and innocent blood.
- reason: Cross-references exposed malformed article-less English; revised wording keeps the local Greek sequence.

## Psalms 88:15
- status: `revised`
- change: `Righteousness and judgment preparation of your throne` -> `Righteousness and judgment are the foundation of your throne`
- reason: The Greek clause needs an English copula; `foundation` captures the throne-establishment sense.

## Proverbs 22:4
- status: `revised`
- change: `Race of wisdom = fear of Lord` -> `The fear of the Lord is the offspring of wisdom`
- reason: `Race of wisdom` was malformed English for the Greek generation/offspring idiom.

## Job 34:10
- status: `revised`
- change: `to disturb the righteous thing` -> `to pervert what is right`
- reason: The phrase refers to corrupting justice/right, not disturbing an object.

## Ezekiel 33:14
- status: `revised`
- change: smoothed the conditional speech formula and kept the verse-final continuation comma.
- reason: The verse continues into the next condition; revised wording removes malformed literal phrasing without closing the sentence.

## Ezekiel 18:27
- status: `revised`
- change: smoothed the turn-from-lawlessness clause.
- reason: The judgment-and-righteousness formula was sound, but the English needed articles and a clearer relative clause.

## Zechariah 14:20
- status: `revised`
- change: smoothed the bridle inscription and vessel comparison.
- reason: The Greek `the thing on the bridle` is readable in English as an inscription/formula while preserving the local bridle reading.

## Ezekiel 43:18
- status: `revised`
- change: smoothed altar-ordinance wording and purpose infinitives.
- reason: The source sense was correct, but article-less literal English made the verse read unfinished.

## Ecclesiastes 7:15
- status: `revised`
- change: smoothed articles and `ungodly man` -> `impious man`.
- reason: Keeps the Greek righteous/impious contrast while making the sentence readable.

## Ecclesiastes 3:16
- status: `revised`
- change: preserved the repeated impious figure in both parallel locations.
- reason: The local Greek repeats `impious`; revised wording clarifies the parallel structure rather than adopting Brenton's second `godly one`.

## Micah 1:2
- status: `revised`
- change: smoothed the call to hear and the witness clause.
- reason: Keeps the local Greek `among you as a witness` against the MT-style `against you` while making the English coherent.

## Song of Solomon 4:16
- status: `revised`
- change: restored north/south as winds and smoothed the garden-spice invitation.
- reason: The verse invokes the north and south winds; revised wording removes the misleading bare directions.
