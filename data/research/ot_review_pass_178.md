# OT Review Pass 178

Focus: clear English defects in article/copula handling and Zechariah's interpreting-angel formula.

- 1 Chronicles 17:7: added articles in "from the fold, from behind the sheep."
- Psalms 46:3: supplied the English verb and rendered φοβερός as "fearsome."
- Micah 4:4: supplied "the" before "mouth of the Lord Almighty."
- Haggai 1:14: supplied "the remnant" and smoothed plural "works" to collective "work."
- Zechariah 1:9, 1:13, 1:14; 2:2, 2:7; 4:1, 4:4, 4:5; 5:5; 6:4, 6:5: changed "angel speaking in me" to "the angel speaking with me" with notes on the Greek formula.
- Zechariah 2:12: supplied "the" in "the pupil of his eye."
- Zechariah 5:10: supplied "the measure."
