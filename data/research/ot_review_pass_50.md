# OT Review Pass 50

Scope: sample-audit readability defects and repeated beatitude formula cleanup.

- Removed the stray trailing quote from Exodus 40:38 and supplied the expected article in `For the cloud was...`.
- Normalized clear English beatitude formulas such as `Blessed man`, `Blessed one`, `Blessed those`, and `Blessed people`.
- Fixed 2 Chronicles 9:7 from `Blessed your men; blessed these servants` to `Blessed are your men; blessed are these servants`.
- Fixed Psalm 113:23 from `Blessed you by Lord` to `Blessed are you by the Lord`.
- Fixed Psalm 145:5 from `Blessed whose helper God of Jacob` to `Blessed is he whose helper is the God of Jacob`.
- Fixed Isaiah 33:21 from `the name of the Lord great to you; place to you will be... nor will vessel go through` to readable English while preserving the LXX structure.
- Fixed repeated `from mouth of the Lord/Jeremiah` to `from the mouth of...`.
- Fixed Cyrus proclamation wording in 2 Chronicles 36:23 and Ezra 1:1 where article loss made the English read broken.
- Fixed missed beatitude cases in Psalms 65:20, 67:36, 111:1; Proverbs 11:25; Ecclesiastes 10:17; Isaiah 31:9; Daniel 12:12; and Zechariah 11:5.
