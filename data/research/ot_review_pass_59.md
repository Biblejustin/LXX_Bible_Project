# OT Review Pass 59

Scope: context-sensitive article cleanup after pass 58.

Applied:

- `from/into/to land which/that/where/about/concerning` -> `from/into/to the land ...`
- `into land dark and gloomy` -> `into a dark and gloomy land`
- `into land like your land` -> `into a land like your land`
- `into land whose bars` -> `into a land whose bars`
- `in/from/to/on wall of` -> `in/from/to/on the wall of`
- `width of wall` -> `width of the wall`
- `upon/over/on head of` -> `upon/over/on the head of`
- `on/upon/in seat of` -> `on/upon/in the seat of`
- `before/from/in tent of` -> `before/from/in the tent of`
- `in eyes of` -> `in the eyes of`

Rationale:

- These were remaining visible English article omissions.
- Land-relative cases were handled with explicit wording because `the land` vs. `a land` depends on context.
- Broader structural phrases not covered here remain for later review.
