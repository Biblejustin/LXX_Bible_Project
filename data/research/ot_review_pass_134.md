# OT Review Pass 134

Scope: obvious English idiom consistency in remaining `like` formulas.

Applied:
- `Act like man` / `act like man` -> `Act like a man` / `act like a man`
- `like birthing woman` -> `like a birthing woman`
- `like son of man` -> `like a son of man`

Rationale:
- Joshua already used `act like a man`; Chronicles, Psalms, and Micah now match that idiom.
- Daniel 7:13 now uses standard English article in the "son of man" simile without changing the LXX reading.
