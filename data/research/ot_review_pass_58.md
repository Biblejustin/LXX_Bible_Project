# OT Review Pass 58

Scope: article cleanup for recurring body/structure idioms.

Applied:

- `from/in/over/against hand of` -> `from/in/over/against the hand of`
- `upon/to/into heart of` -> `upon/to/into the heart of`
- `by/from/to/through/in gate of` -> `by/from/to/through/in the gate of`
- `in/from/to/into court of` -> `in/from/to/into the court of`
- `on/upon throne of` -> `on/upon the throne of`
- `in/on/to/into mouth of` -> `in/on/to/into the mouth of`
- `inside house of` -> `inside the house of`

Rationale:

- These were repeated English article omissions in otherwise stable verses.
- Rules remain scoped to preposition + noun + `of` forms.
- Broader idioms such as `land which...`, `wall of...`, `head of...`, and other context-sensitive phrases remain for later review.
