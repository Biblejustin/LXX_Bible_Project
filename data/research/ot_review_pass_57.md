# OT Review Pass 57

Scope: article cleanup for prepositional place phrases.

Applied:

- `from/into/to/onto land of` -> `from/into/to/onto the land of`
- `from/into/to/in city of` -> `from/into/to/in the city of`
- `from/into/to/in mountain of` -> `from/into/to/in the mountain of`
- `before/from/toward/in temple of` -> `before/from/toward/in the temple of`
- `upon/on/before/to altar of` -> `upon/on/before/to the altar of`
- `before ark of` -> `before the ark of`

Rationale:

- These are English article omissions introduced by overly literal Greek phrase handling.
- The pass does not alter LXX numbering, source order, or textual notes.
- Indefinite cases such as `into land which...` remain for later review because some may need `a land` rather than `the land`.
