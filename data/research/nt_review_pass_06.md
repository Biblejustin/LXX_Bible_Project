# NT TR Review Pass 06

Scope: focused check of high-score rows created by fourth NT cleanup wave.

## Galatians 2:17
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised to servant of sin and May it not be. This preserves diakonos as servant rather than an ecclesial office and renders me genoito without God-language not present in the Greek.

## 1 Peter 5:13
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised to She who is in Babylon, elect together with you. Greek has the feminine article/adjective and no explicit ekklesia noun, so church is not kept in the main line.

## 1 Peter 4:10
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised to serve it to one another. Greek diakonountes is service language tied to the received gift.

## 1 Timothy 1:4
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised to produce disputes. Greek parechousi means produce/provide, so minister questions was misleading modern English.

## Hebrews 13:20
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised everlasting covenant to eternal covenant. Greek has aionios, and this pass standardizes aionios as eternal.

## John 5:24
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised everlasting life to eternal life. Greek has aionios, and this pass standardizes aionios as eternal.
