Scope: safe singular count-noun image formulas remaining after Pass135.

- Object images: `like a beam`, `like a heap`, `like a furnace`, `like a flame of fire`, `like a bronze pot`, `like a stone cube`
- Person/role images that require articles: `like a hired man`, `as a watchman`, `as a shepherd`, `as an enemy`
- Animal/nature images: `like a deaf asp`, `like a flock`, `like a palm tree`, `like a cedar`, `like an oak`
- Song/poetic images: `like a herd`, `as a piece`, `as a seal`, `as a tent`, `as a city`
- Sound formula: `as the sound of`
- Ezekiel sign-act formula: `as an iron wall`

Left unchanged:
- Mass/material comparisons such as dust, fire, wax, mud, water, light, silver, and gold.
- Proper-name boundary formulas after `as far as`.
