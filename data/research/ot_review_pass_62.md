# OT Review Pass 62

Scope: bounded article cleanup for remaining common idiom formulas.

Applied:
- `in day/time/book/words/way/ways/sight/multitude` -> `in the ...`
- `to words/multitude/command` -> `to the ...`
- `by command`, `according to command` -> `by/according to the command`
- `in houses/courts/assembly/plain/reign`, `at beginning`, `at sound` -> article-normalized forms
- `on mountains`, `over/upon land` -> article-normalized forms
- `land of north` -> `land of the north`
- `land of life` -> `land of the living`
- `face of sword` -> `face of the sword`

Rationale:
- These were surfaced by pass 62 scans and are high-confidence English article omissions.
- Continued to avoid broad proper-name constructs and high-risk `hand(s) of`/`sons of` patterns.
