# OT review pass 175

Scope = release sample audit wording cleanup.

## Genesis 27:38
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace physical-sounding `Isaac was pierced` with `when Isaac was cut to the heart`.
- reason: Greek `κατανυχθέντος` can carry pierced/stung force, but English `was pierced` reads as a bodily wound in context.

## Genesis 34:7
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `the men were pierced` with `the men were cut to the heart`.
- reason: Same emotional-piercing verb class as Genesis 27:38; English should not imply a bodily wound.

## Leviticus 10:3
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `Aaron was pierced` with `Aaron was cut to the heart`.
- reason: Preserves the Greek emotional-piercing force and aligns with Brenton's heart-language without making the line sound physical.

## 1 Samuel 2:5
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `the one many in children` with `the one with many children`.
- reason: Same referent, grammatical English.

## 1 Kings 8:59
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `the matter of each day in its day` with `each day's matter on its day`.
- reason: Keeps the day-by-day formula while removing opaque English.

## 1 Kings 20:27
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `Ahab was pierced to the heart from before the Lord` with `Ahab was cut to the heart before the Lord`.
- reason: Keeps emotional-piercing sense and smooths `from before`.

## 1 Kings 20:29
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `Ahab was pierced to the heart from before me` with `Ahab was cut to the heart before me`.
- reason: Same verb and context as 1 Kings 20:27.

## 2 Kings 25:30
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `a matter of a day in its day` with `a day's portion on its day`.
- reason: Keeps the day-by-day provision wording while removing opaque English.

## 2 Chronicles 8:14
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `according to the matter of day in its day` with `according to each day's matter on its day`.
- reason: Keeps the scheduling formula while making the sentence readable.

## Job 19:25
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `the one about to free me` with `the one who is about to free me`.
- reason: Makes the participial phrase complete in English while preserving the literal sequence.

## Isaiah 59:5
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `one about to eat their eggs` with `the one who is about to eat their eggs`.
- reason: Makes the participial phrase complete in English.

## Joel 2:17
- status: `revised`
- decision: Replace `Between the base of the altar` with `At the base of the altar`.
- reason: Preserves the altar-base location while making the sentence grammatical English.
