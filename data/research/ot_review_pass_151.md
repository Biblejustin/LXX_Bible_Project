# OT Review Pass 151

Scope: sentence-initial singular substantives.

Changed:
- Psalm singulars such as `Fool said`, `Righteous one will rejoice`, and `Sinner watches righteous man` now carry normal English articles.
- Proverbial openings such as `Wise son`, `Wise man`, `Fool`, `Lawless man`, and `Ungodly man` now read as `A/The ...` where English requires an article.

Notes:
- Kept generic plural classes such as `lawless men`, `righteous men`, and `poor men` unchanged.
- This pass was exact-string scoped; no broad moral-group rewrite was applied.
