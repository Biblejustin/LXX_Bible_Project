# OT Review Pass 132

Scope: remaining singular count-noun similes and one temple-purpose formula after Pass131.

Applied:
- `like garment/cloak/skin-bag` -> `like a garment/cloak/skin-bag`
- `like cloud/river/wave` -> `like a cloud/river/wave`
- `as woman` -> `as a woman`
- `like dead man/ruined vessel` -> `like a dead man/ruined vessel`
- `as house of sacrifice` -> `as a house of sacrifice`

Left unchanged:
- Mass or material similes such as `like snow`, `like dung`, `like smoke`, `like light`, `like chaff`, `like water`, `like fire`, `like dust`, `like silver`, and `like dew`.
- Office/role formulas such as `as king`, `as priest`, `as wife`, `as head`, and `as father`.
