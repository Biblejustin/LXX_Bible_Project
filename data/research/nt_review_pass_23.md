# NT TR Review Pass 23

Scope: residual queue items introduced by the low-risk grammar/style cleanup pass.

## Acts 11:23
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "cling to the Lord"; Greek has προσμενειν τω κυριω, and "cling/remain attached to" preserves the exhortation while replacing archaic "cleave unto."

## Hebrews 8:9
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "did not continue... did not regard"; Greek has ουκ ενεμειναν and ημελησα, so the modern negative order preserves the sense without changing the covenant claim.

## Matthew 26:22
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "exceedingly sorrowful"; Greek has λυπουμενοι σφοδρα, and this only modernizes the adverbial force of σφοδρα.
