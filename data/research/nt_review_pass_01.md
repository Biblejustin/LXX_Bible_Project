# NT TR Review Pass 01

Scope: high-priority TR literal vs UKJV witness rows. UKJV is witness only.

## Matthew 1:1
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "origin" for geneseos. It preserves source sense better than inherited "generation" while keeping genealogy context visible.

## Mark 1:1
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "good news" for euangelion. "Gospel" is traditional but less transparent as a literal rendering.

## John 1:1
- status: `revised`
- decision: Use "In the beginning" for English clarity and canonical resonance while retaining the Greek opening formula.

## John 1:14
- status: `revised`
- decision: Use "became flesh" for egeneto and "tabernacled among us" for eskenosen. This keeps the incarnation and dwelling imagery explicit.

## Romans 1:1
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "slave" for doulos and "good news" for euangelion. "Called apostle" remains a compressed literal rendering of kletos apostolos.

## Romans 1:3
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "David's seed according to flesh." The wording preserves sperma and sarx without importing explanatory titles from the witness.

## Romans 1:4
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "marked out Son of God" for horisthentos huiou theou. "By resurrection of dead ones" keeps the source phrasing visible.

## Matthew 16:18
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "assembly" for ekklesia and "Hades" for hadou. These are lexical choices, not doctrine-level paraphrase.

## 1 Corinthians 1:2
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "assembly" for ekklesia and "holy ones" for hagiois. "Saints" is traditional but less transparent.

## 1 Corinthians 4:17
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "assembly" for ekklesia. Difference from UKJV is lexical transparency, not alternate sense.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:1
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "assembly" for ekklesia. The opening address remains otherwise aligned with the TR wording.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:8
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "good news" for euangelio. The context is judgment for disobeying the announced message of Christ.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:14
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "good news" for euangeliou. "Gospel" is acceptable tradition, but the draft keeps the Greek compound sense visible.

## John 8:34
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "Amen, amen" and "slave of sin." Both are direct lexical choices from amen and doulos.

## John 13:16
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "Amen, amen" and "slave." The double amen formula and doulos relation are deliberate literal signals.

## Acts 2:31
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "Hades" for hadou. UKJV "hell" collapses a distinct Greek term.

## Acts 2:47
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "assembly" for ekklesia. The verse speaks of the gathered body added by the Lord.

## Acts 20:24
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "good news of the grace of God" for euangelion. The phrase identifies the preached message rather than a later title.

## James 1:1
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "slave" for doulos in James's self-designation. "Servant" is smoother but weaker.

## Philippians 1:1
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "slaves," "holy ones," "overseers," and "servants" for douloi, hagioi, episkopoi, and diakonoi.

## Ephesians 1:13
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "good news of your salvation" and "Spirit of promise." The draft removes UKJV lexical tags and keeps Greek sense.

## Ephesians 5:29
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "assembly" for ekklesia. The image is Christ's care for the gathered body.

## Revelation 1:1
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "slaves" for doulois/doulo and "must happen quickly" for dei genesthai en tachei.

## Revelation 2:18
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "assembly" for ekklesia in the message heading. "Church" remains traditional witness wording.

## Revelation 15:3
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "slave of God" and "King of holy ones." The source uses doulou and hagion.
