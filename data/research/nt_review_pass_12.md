# NT TR Review Pass 12

Scope: focused zoopoieo/syzoopoieo cleanup.

## Ephesians 2:1
- status: `revised`
- decision: Removed supplied "quickened" from this verse. Greek has "you, being dead"; the main verb arrives in Ephesians 2:5.

## Ephesians 2:5
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised to "he made us alive together with Christ." Greek syzoopoieo supports made alive together.

## Colossians 2:13
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised to "he made alive together with him." Greek syzoopoieo supports made alive together.

## 1 Corinthians 15:22
- status: `keep`
- decision: Keep "made alive." Greek zoopoieo supports made alive/give life.

## 1 Corinthians 15:36
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised to "not made alive, unless it dies." Greek zoopoieo supports made alive rather than archaic quickened.

## 1 Peter 3:18
- status: `revised`
- decision: Revised to "made alive in spirit." The flesh/spirit dative pair is kept more literally than UKJV quickened by the Spirit.
