## Number Meanings

- 1: Unity, God’s Sovereignty
  Represents the oneness of God (Deuteronomy 6:4, "The Lord our God is one"). Symbolizes primacy and the beginning.
- 2: Union, Division, or Witness
  Signifies partnership or confirmation by two witnesses (Ecclesiastes 4:9; Matthew 18:16). Can also indicate contrast or division.
- 3: Divine Completeness, Trinity
  Reflects the Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Spirit) and divine perfection (Matthew 28:19; John 21:14, Jesus’ third appearance post-resurrection).
- 4: Creation, Universality
  Associated with the created world (four corners of the earth, four seasons; Revelation 7:1). Represents God’s creative works.
- 5: Grace, God’s Goodness
  Linked to God’s favor and grace (five offerings in Leviticus; Genesis 43:34, five portions for Benjamin). Often tied to human weakness met by divine strength.
- 6: Humanity, Imperfection
  Represents human effort or falling short of divine perfection (created on the sixth day, Genesis 1:26-31; 666 as the number of the beast, Revelation 13:18).
- 7: Perfection, Completion
  Denotes spiritual perfection and God’s completed work (seven days of creation, Genesis 2:2; seven seals in Revelation). Highly significant in Scripture.
- 8: New Beginnings
  Symbolizes renewal or a new order (eight people saved on Noah’s ark, Genesis 7:13; circumcision on the eighth day, Leviticus 12:3). Represents resurrection and "born again".
- 9: Divine Completeness, Finality
  Symbolizes divine completeness or finality (nine fruits of the Holy Spirit, Galatians 5:22-23; nine people with leprosy in Scripture).
- 10: Divine Order, Completeness of Order
  Represents a complete system or human responsibility (Ten Commandments, Exodus 20; ten plagues, Exodus 7-12).
- 11: Disorder, Chaos, Judgment
  Symbolizes disorder or judgment (eleven dukes of Edom; eleven officials offended by truth).
- 12: God’s Government, Authority
  Indicates divine authority or organization (twelve tribes of Israel, Genesis 49:28; twelve apostles, Matthew 10:1-2). Denotes the Creator's foundation or perfect authority
- 13: Rebellion, Lawlessness
  Symbolic of rebellion (Nimrod as generation 13 in Ham's line; thirteen things that defile a person).
- 14: Double Spiritual Perfection
  Multiplies the perfection of 7 (fourteen days for Passover; lineage from Abraham to Jesus in sets of 14).
- 15: Rest After Deliverance
  Symbolizes rest following deliverance (Feast of Unleavened Bread on Nisan 15). Key references: Genesis 15:12-16 (Abraham's vision), Exodus 12:40-42 (Exodus from Egypt).
- 16: Love and Loving
  Represents the duality of true love (16 qualities of God's love in 1 Corinthians 13:4-8). Key references: Genesis 46:18 (Jacob's migration), 1 Corinthians 13:4-8.
- 17: Overcoming the Enemy, Victory
  Represents complete victory (ten + seven; Day of Atonement themes).
- 18: Bondage
  Symbolizes physical or spiritual bondage (Israelites oppressed for 18 years). Key references: Judges 3:14, Luke 13:11-12 (woman healed after 18 years).
- 19: God's Perfect Order in Judgment
  Symbolizes perfect order concerning judgment (10 + 9). Key references: Joshua 19:38 (Naphtali's cities), Psalm 19 (heavens declare God's glory).
- 20: Perfect Waiting
  Symbolizes complete waiting (twice ten; age for Levite service).
- 21: Great Wickedness, Rebellion, and Sin
  Represents an outgrowth of sin and rebellion (21 sins in 2 Timothy 3:1-5). Key references: 2 Timothy 3:1-5, Revelation 21:21 (New Jerusalem).
- 22: Light
  Symbolizes light and revelation (22 Hebrew letters). Key references: Psalms 119:105, John 12:46.
- 23: Divine Protection and Guidance
  Symbolizes protection, blessing, and judgment (Psalm 23). Key references: Psalm 23, 1 Corinthians 10:8 (23,000 fell).
- 24: Priesthood
  Symbolizes the priesthood and worship (24 courses of priests). Key references: 1 Chronicles 23-24, Revelation 4:4 (24 elders).
- 25: Grace Upon Grace
  Symbolizes multiplied grace (25 cubits in Ezekiel's temple). Key references: John 1:16-17, Ezekiel 40:13.
- 26: Eternal God (YHWH)
  Symbolizes God's eternal nature (Gematria of YHWH=26). Key references: Genesis 1:26 (creation in God's image), Exodus (Moses as 26th generation).
- 30: Dedication to a Task
  Represents dedication or maturity (Jesus' ministry at 30; price of betrayal).
- 33: God's Promises
  Connected to divine promises (Jesus' age at death; 33 times "three" in Revelation).
- 40: Testing, Trial, Preparation
  Associated with periods of testing or probation (40 days of rain in Noah’s flood, Genesis 7:12; Jesus’ 40 days in the wilderness, Matthew 4:2).
- 50: Holy Spirit, Pentecost, Deliverance
  Linked to the outpouring of God's Spirit (Pentecost, 50 days after Passover; Jubilee every 50 years;).
- 60: Threshold of Later Life, Pride
  Represents entering life's final phase (threescore years; influences like pride in parables;).
- 70: Completeness, Restoration
  Represents fullness or a significant period (70 years of exile, Jeremiah 25:11; 70 disciples sent out, Luke 10:1).
- 666: Perfection of Imperfection, Beast
  Symbolizes ultimate human imperfection and Satan's system (mark of the Beast, Revelation 13:18)
